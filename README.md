# વિશ્વાસ (Vishwas) - milestone payment tracker for freelancers

Split a project into milestones, share one link with your client, and let the client approve each milestone without logging in. On-time deliveries build your trust score. Gujarati and English.

**Website:** https://1darshankumarsavsani-blip.github.io/Vishwas/

How it works
1. You sign in with Google and create a deal (client, total, milestones with due dates).
2. Send the client link on WhatsApp. The client sees progress and approves delivered milestones.
3. You mark milestones delivered and paid. Trust score = share of milestones delivered on or before the due date.

Honest note: this version does not hold or move money. The client pays you directly (UPI), you mark it received. Online payment collection needs a payment-gateway business account and can be added later. Built by Darshankumar Savsani (DARSHAN CORPORATION).
